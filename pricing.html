Before you book

Run through this list so nothing holds up your first lesson.

  • A valid learner's permit Required before booking You'll be asked to confirm you have one when you book. Lessons can't go ahead without it.
  • Your permit, in person, at every lesson Bring the physical permit, not a photo of it.
  • Glasses or contacts, if you need them to drive Especially if your permit lists a vision restriction.
  • Comfortable, closed-toe shoes They give you a better feel for the pedals than sandals or heels.
  • Registration and insurance, if you're using your car Keep current copies in the vehicle.

Common questions

I've never driven before. Is that okay?

Yes. Lessons are designed for complete beginners too. Your first lesson happens somewhere quiet, where you can learn the controls with no traffic around, and you only move on when you feel ready.

Can I book before I get my learner's permit?

No. You need a valid learner's permit before you book. Once you have it, pick a time on the booking calendar.

How does the highway training work?

You build up to it. Once you're comfortable on quiet streets and in city traffic, Shun takes you onto the highway at quieter times first, then works up to heavier traffic until merging and lane changes feel routine.

When do I pay?

On the day of your lesson. The full lesson price is due that day, plus fuel if you're learning in Shun's car.

How do I change or cancel a lesson?

Reply to your confirmation email or contact Shun directly, as early as you can, so the time can go to another student.

Ready when you are.

Pick a time that suits you. It takes about two minutes.
